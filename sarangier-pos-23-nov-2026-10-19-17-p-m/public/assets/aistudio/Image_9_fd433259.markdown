## Brand & Style

This design system serves modern commerce, inventory management, and POS environments focused on disposable goods, eco-packaging, and institutional hygiene supplies. It balances commercial speed and tactile utility with a distinct organic freshness derived directly from the brand seal.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The visual identity fuses **Modern Clean SaaS / POS Utility** (inspired by systems like Square Terminal, Shopify POS, and Linear) with an **Organic Earth & Botanical Tone**. Rather than relying on sterile, cold grays, it leverages warm bone, warm sand, and deep forest emeralds to communicate cleanliness, environmental consciousness, and commercial trust.

### Emotional Response & Principles
- **Effortless Precision:** Fast transaction speed, unambiguous visual hierarchies, and rapid data comprehension in high-frequency sales environments.
- **Organic Reliability:** Deep emeralds provide brand grounding and institutional stability, while warm sand canvases reduce glare and digital fatigue during long shifts.
- **Physical Tactility:** Generous tap targets (44px minimum touch targets), responsive active states, and tactile surface separation designed for both handheld barcode terminals and high-resolution counter displays.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the physical commodities of the business—cellulose paper, eco-friendly disposables, botanical cleaners, and institutional packaging.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Primary Brand & Income (`#1B4332` / `#2D6A4F`):** Deep emerald forest green. Anchors top navigation, primary actions, positive cash inflow, invoice approvals, and core branding.
- **Secondary / Positive Status (`#52B788`):** Sage leaf. Used for healthy inventory levels, confirmations, order completion tags, and active operational badges.
- **Tertiary / Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber. Dictates low-stock notifications, replenishment alerts, and pending payment holds.
- **Critical & Expense (`#EF4444`):** Soft coral red. Identifies stockouts (`stock = 0`), critical alerts, cancellations, credit notes, and operating expenses.
- **Technical Slate (`#475569`):** Slate blue-gray. Dedicated to operational metadata, SKUs, barcode values, batch numbers, and secondary table columns.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces (`#FAFAF7` / `#F4F1EA`):** Warm bone-linen base. Creates an organic, anti-glare foundation that cleanly contrasts against pure white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Text & Contrast:** Heavy charcoal `#111827` for high-legibility item names, unit values, and cash registers.

## Typography

The typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric precision paired with friendly, approachable terminal counters. 

### Tabular Numbers & Financial Data
All inventory counts, prices, batch identifiers, and stock metrics must strictly employ tabular lining figures:
`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1;`
This guarantees vertical alignment across multi-line order dockets, invoice calculation lines, and stock comparison grids without horizontal jitter.

### Hierarchy & Responsive Adaptations
- **Transaction Totals (`label-numeric-lg`):** Reserved for cart balances, drawer totals, and main KPI figures.
- **Technical Identifiers (`label-mono-sku`):** Rendered uppercase with generous tracking (`0.04em`) in Slate Blue (`#475569`) to distinguish product codes from standard descriptions.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** For mobile handheld scanners and smaller terminal screens, `headline-xl` scales down to `headline-lg` (`1.75rem`), preserving vertical canvas for lists and checkout carts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a **density-adaptive fluid grid** built for POS registers, stockroom tablets, and administrative desktop consoles.

### Grid Rhythm & Screen Breakpoints
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 599px):** Single-column layout. Margin `1rem`, gutter `0.75rem`. Sticky checkout bottom bar; full-width product scanning cards.
- **Counter Tablet / Split POS (600px - 1023px):** Two-column operational split (60% item catalogue grid, 40% persistent ticket/cart panel). Margin `1.25rem`, gutter `1rem`.
- **Desktop Backoffice (1024px+):** Max canvas `1440px` centered. 12-column grid for inventory matrices, reorder analytics, and batch management. Margin `2rem`, gutter `1.25rem`.

### Touch-First Density Rules
Vertical tap rhythm is prioritized over extreme compactness:
- Interactive rows maintain an explicit minimum footprint of `44px` to `48px` to prevent mis-taps in fast-paced retail or warehouse dispatch stations.
- Spacing between adjacent interactive tiles must never drop below `space-sm` (`0.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **tonal surface layering** combined with **subtle, organic ambient shadows** and crisp micro-borders. High-gloss or pure skeuomorphic treatments are avoided in favor of crisp, modern POS ergonomics.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`Level 0`):** `#F4F1EA` (warm sand-linen). Defines the global floor.
- **Structural Containers (`Level 1`):** `#FFFFFF` (crisp white). Used for cards, grid items, and table sheets. Border: `1px solid rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Flyout Drawers & Modals (`Level 2`):** `#FFFFFF`. Floating cart summaries, tender dialogue modals, and barcode scanning popups. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Sticky POS Bar / Action Strip (`Level 3`):** Fixed bottom/side tender execution bars. Casts an upward diffused shadow tinted in primary green: `0 -4px 16px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`.

### Border Discipline
All surfaced containers feature a delicate 1px boundary using muted botanical tones (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)` or `rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.12)`), ensuring structural edge clarity under harsh retail or warehouse lighting.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **soft-rounded physical curvature** (10px to 14px for standard containers) that reflects modern hardware terminals while mirroring the circular, organic curves of the brand identity.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Base Components (Inputs, Table Rows, Standard Buttons):** `rounded-md` (`0.625rem` / `10px`).
- **Cards, POS Tiles, and Modals:** `rounded-lg` (`0.875rem` / `14px`). Softens large operational surfaces without sacrificing data density.
- **Status Badges, Stock Pills, and Quantity Counters:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) to immediately segregate metadata tags from actionable rectangular inputs.

## Components

### Buttons & Quick-Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (Tender / Register / Save):** Minimum height `48px`. Background `#1B4332`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `10px`. Hover: `#2D6A4F`. Active/Pressed: transform scale down `0.98` with `#143326`.
- **Secondary (Add Item / Split Bill):** Height `44px`. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #2D6A4F`, text `#1B4332`.
- **Destructive / Void (`stock = 0` / Clear Order):** Soft coral `#EF4444` background or `#FEE2E2` tint with `#DC2626` text.

### Product & Stock Cards (POS Grid)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `14px`, padding `1rem`. Includes a defined image/icon slot for disposable/hygiene items (e.g., cups, roll towels, detergents).
- Top right features the status pill badge:
  - In Stock: `#ECFDF5` background, `#2D6A4F` text.
  - Low Stock: `#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` text.
  - Out of Stock: `#FEE2E2` background, `#DC2626` text.
- Price displayed in `label-numeric-lg` with tabular alignment (`$ 12.500`).

### Numeric Quantity Stepper
- Integrated `+` / `–` button unit with minimum hit areas of `44x44px`. Tabular number displayed centrally in high-contrast charcoal `#111827`.

### Input & Search Fields
- Height `48px`. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.2)`, text `#111827`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. 
- Focus state: Border `#2D6A4F` with a soft emerald halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.15)`.

### Operational Data Tables
- Header row styled in `#F4F1EA` with uppercase metadata tracking (`label-mono-sku`).
- Alternating row striping disabled in favor of clean 1px bottom divider lines (`#E2E8F0`).
- Strict right-alignment for SKU numbers, physical inventory counts, and financial totals using tabular numeral settings.
